# Your cellar and its shelf map

What you hold, where it sits, and what it's worth.

## Adding bottles

**+ Add purchase** (on the Home page, or **+ Bottle** on a wine's page, or *Add bottle* in ⌘K): the wine, then the
bottles: how many, their size, the purchase price and date, the vendor, the provenance (an importer, a seller, who
brought it) and the **location**. A location
named after a storage group's prefix (`H4` for Home's prefix `H`) goes in that group's bin 4.

A new wine can start from a link: paste its page at a shop or on Vivino in **Fill from a link** (at the top of the new
wine's fields), and its producer, cuvée, vintage, colour and appellation are filled in; a cuvée you already have
brings its spelling, grapes and LWIN, as a new vintage of it. The page's picture and price show beside, to check. Some
shops don't let apps read their pages: then fill it in by hand.

## The views

- **Cards** and **List**: every line in stock, with its value (the list sorts by any column).
- **Shelf map**: each storage group's bins, filled against their capacity and coloured by the regions in them.
  Click a bin to filter the cellar to it; click again to clear.
- **Vintages**: a heatmap of what you hold, region by vintage.
- **Price changes**: the market prices that moved.

The filters (colour, region, producer, appellation, grape, vintage, storage, bin) live in the address, so a filtered
view can be bookmarked. **Grape** finds a wine by any of its grapes, a blend's too.

## A wine list to print

**Wine list** (at the top of the cellar) lays the wines out like a restaurant's list, ready to print or save as a
PDF: sparkling, whites, rosés, reds, then sweet and fortified; by region and appellation; one line per cuvée with its
vintages, the format when it isn't a 75 cl bottle, no prices. It takes the cellar's filters, so filter first for a
selection (what's at home, the reds for tonight), or open it unfiltered for the whole cellar. Give it a title and a
line under it; leave a wine out with its ✕, or one vintage by clicking it. Like the filters, all of it is in the
address: bookmark a list to make it again.

## The wishlist

**Wishlist** (in the menu) keeps the wines you'd like. Add one from its page (**☆ Wishlist**), or find it on the
list's own search: **＋ New vintage** for another vintage of a wine you know, **＋ New wine…** for one not in WineLib
yet (created with it, producer too). Each one takes its size, a note and where to find it; **Any vintage** when the
cuvée is what matters. The list says when you already have some in the cellar. With an LWIN, its market price comes
with the cellar's (**Market prices** in Maintenance, nightly as well), or now with its own **Market price** link.

## Values and market prices

A bottle's value is its market price when it has one, else what you paid. Market prices come by the wine's **LWIN**
(the wine trade's code): Bordeaux Index and WineLabs quote them, with your own keys (Settings › Integrations).
Settings › Maintenance › **LWIN codes** finds the LWIN of wines without one (and gives it to their other vintages);
**Market prices** refreshes them (nightly as well).
