# Tasting notes

Every wine you drink, where and with what.

## Adding a tasting

**+ Add tasting** (on the Home page, or **+ Tasting** on a wine's or a place's page, **Drink** on a bottle's, *Add
tasting note* in ⌘K, **+ Wine** on an occasion's card for another wine that evening):

- **From the cellar**: pick the bottle; one bottle leaves the cellar.
- **Other wine**: a wine you didn't buy (a restaurant, a friend's).

Then the date, the place, your notes and score, how many glasses, the order in the evening (*sequence*) and tags
("Dinner, Gift, corked"). Photos go with it.

Tastings of the same day at the same place make one **occasion**: one card in the journal, its wines in order.
A restaurant meal without wine to note is a **Restaurant visit**.

## Places

A place is a restaurant, a bar, a winery, a home: its city and country, its type, its Michelin stars. A friend's
home is a **Friend's Place**, named after who lives there and shown as a house, who and the city: type **@** and
their name ("@Mia") wherever you name a new place. Its
coordinates: **Search OpenStreetMap** in its form, a Google Maps link pasted in, or a click on the map; Settings ›
Maintenance › **Place coordinates** finds the missing ones.

## The views

- **Cards**: the journal, occasion by occasion (*Notes inline* shows the notes in the cards).
- **List**: every tasting, sortable.
- **Map**: where you drank, place by place.
- **Trends**: what you drink, year by year.

The period (this year, the last 30 days, all time or your own dates) and the filters narrow everything.

## Share a tasting

**Share** on a tasting's page makes a sticker for a story, Strava-style: the wine, your score, when and where; or
the whole evening, its wines in order. A transparent PNG (white or dark text) to lay over a photo, or a solid card.
On a phone, **Share…** sends it to Instagram or anywhere; elsewhere, **Download PNG**.
