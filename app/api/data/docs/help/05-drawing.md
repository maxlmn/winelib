# Drawing an outline

For a lieu-dit, a monopole, an appellation no source outlines, or one you want finer.

## On an appellation or a vineyard page

**Draw outline** draws on the page's own map: it goes full size, on the satellite photos, where you were looking
(zoom first to the place you want to draw), the tools over it:

- **Draw**: click each corner; click the first point (or press Enter) to close the shape. Several shapes make one
  outline (a vineyard in two parts).
- **Edit**: click a shape, drag its points; drag a middle point to add one, right-click a point to delete it.
- **Start from the official outline**: the source's outline as a starting point, to refine.
- **Import file...**: a GeoJSON or KML file (from Google Earth, a GIS, another app).
- **Delete shape** and **Clear**; **Satellite** shows the vines themselves.

**Save outline** keeps it in your database: the map shows yours instead of the source's. Removing it brings the
source's back.

## A vineyard from a wine

A wine without a vineyard has **Draw vineyard**, on the wine's map the same way: name the vineyard, draw it,
**Create vineyard**. The vineyard is created and linked to the wine and to its other vintages. **Cancel** puts the
map back as it was.
