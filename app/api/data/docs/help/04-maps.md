# The maps

The world's appellations, down to the vineyard, with your wines on them.

## The Map page

It opens on one outline per wine region, in the region's colour. Click a region (or pick it in the list) to see its
appellations and vineyards; tick the ones to draw. **My cellar** and **My tastings** keep only where your wines come
from. **Plain** and **Satellite** change the background.

## On the pages

- A **wine** shows its appellation and its vineyard. Click a name in the legend to zoom to its outline; untick
  *Appellations* or *Vineyards* to hide them.
- A **producer** shows every appellation and vineyard of its wines.
- A **region** shows where what you drink comes from, its top appellations and producers.
- An **appellation** or a **vineyard** shows its own outline; **Full detail** draws it as the source does (INAO's
  vine parcels one by one) instead of simplified.

## Where the outlines come from

INAO for France (appellations, complementary geographic names, IGPs, premiers crus), the EU's PDO register, UC
Davis's AVAs, Wine Australia's GIs, Natural Earth for the regions' overview. Settings › Maintenance › **Map
outlines** downloads them; an outline no source has, you can draw (see *Drawing an outline*). Their credits:
Settings › About the data.
