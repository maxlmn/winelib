# Grapes and blends

A wine's grapes, and the grapes' families.

## A wine's grapes

In the wine form, **Grapes** is a list: the main grape first, others after, each with its share in % when you know
it (↑ moves one up). Type any name a grape goes by: *Shiraz* finds Syrah, *Spätburgunder* Pinot Noir.
**Blend** is the style when there is one: Bordeaux, Rhône, Champagne, Field blend (grapes planted and fermented
together) or Other blend.

A grape's page lists every wine it's in, not only those it leads; the cellar's and the tastings' **Grape** filter
find a blend by any of its grapes.

## The Grapes page

Every grape with its colour, origin, parents, offspring and your wines, bottles and tastings; filters by colour,
origin and what you have.

**Family network** draws every known parent → offspring link: dots coloured by the berries, sized by your wines,
a gold ring for those in your cellar. Hover a grape to see its family; click it for its page; wheel to zoom, drag to
move. The filters keep their grapes; a relative they leave out is a small hollow dot.

## A grape's page

Its profile (climate, acidity, tannin, aromas, the classic regions), its description from Wikipedia, and its
**family tree**: grandparents, parents, offspring. The parentage is VIVC's (the Vitis International Variety
Catalogue), confirmed by DNA.
