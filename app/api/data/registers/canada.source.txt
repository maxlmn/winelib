ONTARIO
Source: Government of Ontario, e-Laws (official consolidated law)
Document: O. Reg. 406/00, "Rules of Vintners Quality Alliance Ontario Relating to Terms for VQA Wine", under the Vintners Quality Alliance Act, 1999, S.O. 1999, c. 3
Version used: consolidation period July 6, 2026 - e-Laws currency date October 21, 2026 (last amendment O. Reg. 226/26); accessed 2026-10-25
URL: https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/000406
Content used: s. 2 and Table 1 "Viticultural Areas" (items 1-17), cross-checked against s. 3 (2) list of protected terms (items 3-20).
Supporting (hierarchy terminology only): VQA Ontario, https://vqaontario.ca/ontario-appellations/niagara-peninsula/west-niagara/ (West Niagara described as the third regional appellation within the Niagara Peninsula, after Niagara Escarpment and Niagara-on-the-Lake).
Mapping notes:
  - "Ontario" is the provincial geographical indication (s. 3 (2) item 3; s. 3 (6) (d) "the geographical indication Ontario"); included as top-level row, type "Provincial appellation".
  - DVAs (type "DVA"): Niagara Peninsula, Lake Erie North Shore, Prince Edward County, South Islands.
  - Table 1 items 4-6 (Niagara Escarpment, Niagara-on-the-Lake, West Niagara) are defined as unions of sub-viticultural areas; typed "Regional appellation" (VQA Ontario's term), within Niagara Peninsula. West Niagara was added by O. Reg. 359/24.
  - The ten sub-viticultural areas (type "Sub-appellation") are assigned to Niagara-on-the-Lake or West Niagara, which together partition them exactly. Niagara Escarpment overlaps: it consists of Beamsville Bench, Twenty Mile Bench, Short Hills Bench (also West Niagara) and St. David’s Bench (also Niagara-on-the-Lake). Because "within" is single-valued, this second membership is not encoded.
  - The regulation spells "St. David’s Bench" with a typographic apostrophe; straight-apostrophe spelling given as alias.
Licence/terms: published by the King's Printer for Ontario on e-Laws; official legal text (Ontario permits reproduction of e-Laws legislation; no specific licence line on the page).

NOVA SCOTIA
"Tidal Bay" is NOT included: it is a wine-style appellation whose standards are set and administered by the Winery Association of Nova Scotia (industry body; launched 2012). No provincial or federal regulation designating it as a geographical indication was found.
