Selection: 33 of the 44 AOs are for wine. Excluded as non-wine: Tushetian Guda (cheese), Nabeghlavi, Sairme, Borjomi,
  Mitarbi, Skuri, Utsera, Bolnisi (mineral water), Zvare (mineral waters), Askhi Mountain Sulguni, Narchvi (cheeses).
Names: register's Latin spelling (case normalised from upper case); register qualifiers dropped where only
  disambiguating ("Bolnisi (wine)" -> Bolnisi); additional signs in brackets given as aliases (Kakhuri, Atenuri, Akhmetis Mtsvane).
within: left empty - the registry list does not state a historical region (the specifications describe locations,
  but "Kakheti", "Racha", "Lechkhumi" are themselves AOs, so a region value would be ambiguous).
