Mapping notes:
  - "within" = the department/region named in the INV list's "Departamento y Provincia" column or the recognising resolution, only where that unit is itself a listed IG (e.g. Agrelo -> Luján de Cuyo, Maipú -> Mendoza). Mendoza, San Juan and La Rioja Argentina are set within Cuyo because the INV list describes Cuyo as the region including those three provinces. Valle de Uco within Mendoza (INV: departments San Carlos, Tunuyán, Tupungato of Mendoza).
  - Duplicate names exist officially: Rivadavia, San Carlos, San Martín (Mendoza and San Juan/Salta), Sarmiento (San Juan dept IG and Chubut 2023 IG), and Luján de Cuyo / San Rafael (each both IG and DOC). Duplicates carry a "(Province)" alias; "within" references to these names are ambiguous by name alone (children of San Carlos-Mendoza: Paraje Altamira, La Consulta, Pampa El Cepillo, Cordón El Cepillo; of San Carlos-Salta: Valle de Pucará - Salta; of Rivadavia-Mendoza: Reducción; of Sarmiento-San Juan: Valle del Pedernal; IG Luján de Cuyo is the parent of Agrelo, Las Compuertas, Vistalba, Alto Agrelo).
  - Cafayate: INV Disposición 08/2005 authorises only "Valle de Cafayate" on labels (Cafayate is a registered trademark).
