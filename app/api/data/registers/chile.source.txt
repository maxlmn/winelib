Mapping notes:
  - Region names are given in short label form (e.g. "Valle Central") with the decree's full form ("Región Vitícola del Valle Central") as alias.
  - Zapallar and Valle del Marga-Marga are areas placed directly in the Aconcagua region (not within a subregion), per Art. 1 No. 3 and the table.
  - Lo Abarca, Cartagena, Algarrobo are areas of Valle de San Antonio; San Juan and Santo Domingo are areas of the Valle de Leyda zone.
  - Rapa Nui - Isla de Pascua is a stand-alone area outside any region (Art. 1 No. 7).
  - Non-standard types: "Special DO" (Secano Interior, Art. 3º Bis) and "Mention" (Andes / Entre Cordilleras / Costa, Art. 5º bis, complementary label information tied to listed areas, not separate territories).
  - Aliases include unaccented spellings (as in the uppercase table) and common short/English forms ("Elqui", "Elqui Valley").
