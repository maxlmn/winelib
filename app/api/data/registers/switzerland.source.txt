Content: 63 AOC in total (register count). Columns: AOC cantonale / AOC régionale / AOC locale.
  In the CSV, the register's cantonal-level AOC are typed "AOC (canton)" (incl. Rosso/Bianco/Rosato del Ticino, which
  the register lists as cantonal); regional and local AOC are typed "AOC". Names as spelled in the register.
  Intercantonal AOC: Vully (Vaud + Fribourg) and Zürichsee (Zürich + Schwyz) are written once (within = Vaud / Zürich).
  Fribourg has no cantonal AOC in the register; Cheyres has within = "Fribourg" (no Fribourg row exists).
